Why your smartwatch shows ⬜⬜⬜ instead of Bangla text

Most smartwatch and smart band fonts — Mi Band, Redmi Band, Xiaomi Smart Band, Amazfit, Galaxy Watch, Pixel Watch — don't include Bengali, Hindi, Tamil or Urdu scripts. When a WhatsApp message, bKash/bank alert or operator SMS arrives in your language, the watch renders empty boxes (tofu) or cuts the message off. NotiMorph fixes that with AI, right on your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Everything people ask about fixing smartwatch notifications with AI.

Why does my smartwatch show boxes or squares instead of Bangla text?
Most watch fonts don't include Bengali, Hindi, Tamil or Urdu scripts, so those characters render as empty boxes ("tofu"). NotiMorph intercepts the notification and rewrites it with AI into readable English or Banglish (Bengali typed in English letters) before your watch sees it.
What exactly is NotiMorph?
A free, open-source Android app that listens to notifications from apps you choose, sends the text to an AI model (Google Gemini, Groq, or any OpenAI-compatible API), and reposts a cleaned version with the original app's icon — fixing garbled text, translating languages, shortening long messages and extracting OTP codes.
Is NotiMorph free?
Yes — MIT-licensed and 100% free. The AI providers have generous free tiers: Gemini keys from aistudio.google.com, Groq keys from console.groq.com.
Does it work with Mi Band, Galaxy Watch, Amazfit, Pixel Watch?
Yes — anything that mirrors Android notifications: Mi Band 4–8, Redmi bands, Xiaomi bands, Galaxy Watch 4–7, Pixel Watch 1–3, Amazfit, TicWatch, Noise, boAt, realme and more.
Does it need root?
No. Only the standard Android "Notification access" permission — the app guides you there on first launch.
Will it drain my battery?
The AI call only runs when a notification from an enabled app arrives. NotiMorph pre-warms its connection and typically finishes a rewrite in under two seconds with minimal battery impact.
Why isn't my OTP or bank notification rewritten on Android 15?
Android 15 hides OTP codes and banking amounts from side-loaded notification-listener apps, delivering "Sensitive notification content hidden" instead. NotiMorph detects this and keeps the original. Turning off the system "Sensitive notifications / Enhanced notifications" toggle restores full functionality. Android 14 and below are unaffected.
Is my data private?
API keys never leave your phone. Notification text is sent only to the AI provider you choose, purely to rewrite the message. No analytics, no tracking, no accounts — and every rewrite is logged in History so you can see exactly what was sent.
